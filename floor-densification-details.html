Floor Densification

Industrial Floor Hardening

Floor Densification kisi bhi facility ke liye ek practical, decorative aur economical floor system hai. Ye tezi se badhne wala product banta ja raha hai. Floor densification ek multi-step mechanical grinding aur polishing process hai, jisme industrial diamonds aur liquid hardener ka upyog kiya jata hai taaki floor ko level, densify, aur polish kiya ja sake. Ant mein, ye floor surface ko andar se puri tarah seal kar deta hai.
