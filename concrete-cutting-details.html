Diamond Concrete Cutting

Precision Demolition & Renovation

Diamond Concrete Cutting is a high-precision, heavy-duty cutting technique used to create clean and controlled openings in reinforced concrete, brickwork, and stone. Unlike traditional methods, diamond cutting uses specialized industrial-grade diamond blades to "grind" through the hardest materials with surgical accuracy.

This method is essential for modern construction where structural integrity must be maintained. It is a dust-free, vibration-free process that ensures the surrounding concrete remains undamaged.

Key Benefits

Extreme Precision

Delivers perfectly straight edges that require little to no finishing.

Vibration-Free

Prevents micro-cracks from forming in the rest of the structure.

Dust-Free (Wet Cutting)

Water suppress dust and cools the blade, safe for indoor environments.

High Speed & Efficiency

Cuts through heavy steel reinforcement much faster than any other method.

Low Noise

Significantly quieter than traditional demolition methods.

Core Techniques

Wall Sawing

Used for vertical or steep surfaces to create precise openings for doors, windows, and HVAC ducts.

Floor (Slab) Sawing

Ideal for cutting trenches for pipes, expansion joints, and removing large sections of pavement.

Wire Sawing

Flexible diamond wire used for cutting extremely large or complex structures where blades cannot reach.

Core Drilling

Creating perfectly circular holes for plumbing, electrical cables, and structural testing.

Where It Is Used

Creating HVAC & Plumbing Voids
