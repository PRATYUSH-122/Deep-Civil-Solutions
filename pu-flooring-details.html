PU Concrete Flooring

Extreme Industrial Durability

PU Concrete flooring is a high-performance, heavy-duty flooring solution specifically engineered for the most demanding industrial environments. It is a hybrid system that combines the strength of cement with the chemical resistance and flexibility of polyurethane.

This flooring is designed to withstand extreme thermal shocks, heavy impact, and aggressive chemical exposure. It is the gold standard for facilities that require a seamless, hygienic, and incredibly durable surface.

Key Benefits

Thermal Shock Resistance

Ideal for areas subject to extreme temperature changes like cold storage or steam cleaning.

High Chemical Resistance

Protects against organic acids, alkalis, fuels, and heavy solvents effectively.

Superior Durability

High abrasion and impact resistance for heavy forklift and machinery traffic.

Hygienic & Anti-Microbial

Seamless finish prevents bacterial growth, perfect for food and pharma industries.

Moisture Tolerant

Can be applied on relatively new concrete with higher moisture content.
