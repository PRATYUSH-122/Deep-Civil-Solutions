Grouting Solutions

Restore Structural Integrity

Grouting is a specialized engineering process used to fill voids, cracks, and honeycombs in concrete structures to restore their structural integrity and prevent water seepage. By injecting high-strength fluids into gaps, we ensure that the structure remains dense, stable, and protected from environmental damage.

Whether it is for strengthening a weak foundation, stopping active water leaks, or repairing cracks in industrial slabs, our grouting solutions provide a permanent and cost-effective fix for both new and old constructions.

Key Benefits

High Structural Strength

Fills internal gaps to ensure the concrete can carry its intended load safely.

Waterproofing & Sealing

Effectively blocks water flow through cracks, joints, and porous concrete.

Non-Shrink Properties

Our materials do not shrink, ensuring a tight and permanent bond.

Chemical Resistance

Protects reinforcement steel from corrosion by sealing out moisture.

Precision Application

Low-viscosity fluids can penetrate even the smallest micro-cracks.

Grouting Techniques

Epoxy Injection Grouting

High-strength resin injected into fine cracks to weld the concrete back together.

Cementitious Pressure Grouting

Pumping a cement-based slurry into large voids or soil to stabilize foundations.

PU (Polyurethane) Foam Injection

Rapid-expanding foam used specifically to stop high-pressure water leakages instantly.

Micro-Concrete Grouting

Used for heavy-duty repairs where high flowability and strength are required for thick sections.
