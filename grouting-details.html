Grouting Solutions

Restore Structural Integrity

Grouting ek advanced civil engineering process hai jisse hum concrete ke andar ke voids, cracks aur honeycombs ko fill karte hain. Hum high-pressure injection ki madad se gaps ko seal karte hain taaki aapka structure mazboot aur leak-proof bana rahe.

1. Pressure Injection

Hum specialized pumps ka use karke micro-cracks mein epoxy ya cementitious grout inject karte hain jo andar tak seal karta hai.

  • High Strength Repair
  • Leakage Prevention

2. Surface Finishing

Void filling ke baad surface ko properly seal kiya jata hai taaki moisture reinforcement steel tak na pahunche.

  • No Shrinkage
  • Long Term Stability
