Structure Strengthening & Repairs

Restore & Enhance Stability

Structure strengthening ek advanced process hai jisme purane dhanchon ki load-bearing capacity ko badhaya jata hai. Deterioration, purane building codes, ya extra load ke liye ye bohot zaroori hai.

1. Jacketing & Micro Concrete

Columns aur beams ko extra support dene ke liye hum jacketing aur high-strength micro concrete ka upyog karte hain.

  • Increased Load Capacity
  • Long-term Durability

2. Fiber Wrapping (Carbon/Glass)

High-tech Carbon aur Glass fiber wrapping se bina vajan badhaye structure ki taqat kai guna badh jati hai.

  • Lightweight Solution
  • Corrosion Resistant

3. Epoxy & Polymer Repairs

Cracks ko seal karne ke liye epoxy injection grouting aur polymer modified mortar ka use kiya jata hai.

  • Seamless Crack Filling
  • Restored Integrity
