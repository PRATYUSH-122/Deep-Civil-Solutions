1. Acrylic & Polymer Treatments

Advanced polymers ka use karke hum flexible aur durable layer banate hain jo leaks ko jad se khatam karta hai.

  • Flexible Crack Bridging
  • UV Resistant Coating

2. APP & Liquid Membrane

Heaving duty protection ke liye liquid applied aur APP modified membranes ka upyog jo terrace aur heavy industrial floors ke liye best hai.

  • High Tensile Strength
  • Zero Water Penetration

3. Epoxy & Polymer Injection

Deep structural cracks ko seal karne ke liye high-pressure injection grouting technology.

  • Structural Integrity Restoration
  • Permanent Crack Sealing
